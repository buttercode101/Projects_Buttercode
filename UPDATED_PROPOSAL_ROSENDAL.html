Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE)

Startup Funding Proposal • Rosendal, Free State • May 26, 2026

Executive Summary

REE is a small startup focused on guided horse experiences for Rosendal weekend visitors. This plan is conservative by design: small launch, controlled risk, realistic pricing, and growth only after data proves demand.

Scenic Ride
60 minutes

75–90 minutes

10–15 minutes

Operating days: Friday–Sunday + public holidays.

Recent & Reasonable Pricing (2025–2026 aligned)

Pony: R120–R180
Scenic 60m: R380–R520 pp
Sunset: R550–R750 pp
Private Couple: R1,300–R1,900

Funding Ask

R280,000
  • 28% safety gear/tack/setup
  • 20% horse care reserve
  • 15% insurance/compliance
  • 15% marketing + booking setup
  • 12% working capital buffer
  • 10% route/site preparation

Go-to-Market (Small-Town Fit)

Primary channel is accommodation partners in and around Rosendal. Secondary support via WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, and social content.

Safety & Risk Controls

  • Indemnities, rider screening, briefings, and incident procedure
  • Public liability cover and welfare logs
  • Weather cancellation/reschedule policy
  • Lean fixed costs to protect early cashflow

Milestones (First 12 Months)

  1. Months 1–2: compliance + partner onboarding + soft launch
  2. Months 3–6: stabilize weekends and refine pricing from real bookings
  3. Months 7–12: improve occupancy, then add one premium add-on only if margins support it
