1) Executive Summary

REE is a boutique tourism startup designed for Rosendal’s market reality. The business starts small, operates professionally, and scales only after demand and margins are proven.

2) Business Objective

Build Rosendal’s trusted horseback experience provider through reliable service, partner-led demand, and disciplined cashflow management.

3) Market Fit

  • Visitor-driven demand (not resident volume)
  • Weekend concentration supports focused scheduling
  • Accommodation partners need activity offerings for guests

4) Launch Products

5) Practical Launch Pricing

Pony: R120–R180
Scenic (60m): R380–R520 pp
Sunset: R550–R750 pp
Private Couple: R1,300–R1,900

Pricing is set for realistic startup adoption while protecting margins.

6) Funding Ask and Allocation

R280,000
  • 28% safety gear, tack, setup
  • 20% horse care reserve
  • 15% insurance and compliance
  • 15% marketing + booking setup
  • 12% working capital buffer
  • 10% route readiness/signage

7) Operating Model

  • Operating days: Friday–Sunday + public holidays
  • Pre-booking priority and weather-led controls
  • Low fixed overhead and weekly performance tracking

8) Go-to-Market Plan

Primary: accommodation referral partnerships. Secondary: WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, Instagram/Facebook.

9) Safety, Compliance, and Welfare

  • Indemnities and rider screening
  • Mandatory pre-ride briefings
  • Public liability cover
  • Incident protocol and welfare logs

10) Risk Management

  • Weather: rescheduling policy and scheduling buffers
  • Demand swings: partner-led funnel and flexible staffing
  • Cashflow pressure: strict cost and spend controls

11) 12-Month Milestones

Months 1–2: compliance finalization, partner onboarding, soft launch.
Months 3–6: stabilize occupancy and optimize conversion.
Months 7–12: improve repeats/referrals and add one premium upsell if margins allow.
