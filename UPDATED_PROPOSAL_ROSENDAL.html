1) Executive Investment Case

RECE is engineered as a high-discipline venture to capture demand for authentic countryside experiences. It is intentionally structured as a professionally managed platform—not a lifestyle hobby operation—built on governance, safety assurance, and partner-distributed demand.

Demand Thesis

Drive-to domestic tourism is increasingly experience-led. Visitors want packaged stays + activities.

Supply Gap

Few consistently bookable, safety-governed equestrian products in micro-destinations.

Model Edge

Accommodation-embedded distribution + product curation + unit economics control.

Scale Logic

Launch in Rosendal, prove discipline, then replicate playbook in adjacent towns.

2) Opportunity Context: Why Rosendal, Why Now

  • Scenic identity and low-density destination fit.
  • Existing accommodation base supports partner-driven conversion.
  • Weekend and holiday demand concentration aligns with curated ride slots.
  • Consumers increasingly value restorative, story-rich, social-shareable moments.

Commercial reality: demand must come primarily from visitors and partner stays—not local resident foot traffic.

3) Venture Design: Business Model and Value Proposition

Vision

Value Proposition

  • Professionally guided equestrian experiences.
  • Safety-led operations with reliable service standards.
  • Family-friendly and premium occasion products.
  • Easy booking and strong communication workflows.

7) Commercial Strategy

Primary Channel: Accommodation partnerships

  • Commission model: 15%–25% (volume-linked).
  • Partner sales kit: scripts, cards, media, booking links.
  • "Book with your stay" packaging to maximize conversion.

Secondary Channels

  • Google Business Profile
  • Instagram / Facebook short-form
  • WhatsApp booking funnel

8) Operating Model: Safety, Welfare, Standards

Safety Protocols Non-Negotiable

Rider screening, briefings, gear checks, guide ratios, emergency SOPs.

Legal/Compliance

Indemnities, minors consent, public liability insurance, incident logs.

Animal Welfare

Vet/farrier schedules, rest-cycle caps, documented welfare records.

Service QA

On-time departures, post-ride feedback loop, weekly coaching.

9) Financial Model: Assumptions & Return Logic

All figures are planning estimates pending final supplier quotations and pilot validation.

  • Indicative startup envelope: R450,000–R900,000.
  • Portfolio margin discipline: maintain >45% gross margin at product portfolio level.
  • Scaling rule: no product expands without sustained positive contribution margin.

Monthly Revenue Formula

(Scenic × Price) + (Sunset × Price) + (Pony × Price) + (Private × Price) + Add-ons

11) Risk Register & Mitigation

Weather volatility Risk

Dynamic scheduling, transparent cancellation terms, weather alerts.

Seasonality Risk

Portfolio diversity + concentration on high-yield demand windows.

Safety incident Risk

SOP rigor, trained guides, documentation discipline, insurance cover.

Animal health Risk

Preventive care cycles, welfare monitoring, contingency rostering.

13) 24-Month Roadmap

Phase 0 (0–2 months): Compliance pack, SOP finalization, insurance, route tests, partner onboarding.
Phase 1 (3–6 months): Controlled soft launch, slot optimization, review capture, partner acceleration.
Phase 2 (7–12 months): Utilization and basket-value improvements, premium add-ons, referral cadence.
Phase 3 (13–24 months): Expansion readiness testing, replication playbook, growth-round prep.

15) Closing Investment Rationale

RECE combines rural tourism demand with strong operational discipline. The venture is designed to deliver both investable returns and measurable local impact through a staged, safety-first, partnership-powered execution model.

One-line proposition: Fund a governance-led countryside experience platform with credible margin potential and replicable expansion upside.
