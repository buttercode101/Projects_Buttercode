# Startup Funding Proposal
## Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE)
### Professional Investor Version

**Date:** May 26, 2026  
**Location:** Rosendal, Free State, South Africa  
**Capital sought:** R280,000

---

## 1) Executive Summary
Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE) is a boutique tourism startup designed to deliver premium, safety-led horseback activities in Rosendal. The strategy is deliberately conservative: launch lean, protect cashflow, build demand through accommodation partnerships, and scale only when metrics support growth.

This proposal is built for investors seeking:
- Clear use-of-funds discipline
- Practical startup scope
- Measured risk controls
- Professional operations in a small market

---

## 2) Business Objective
Establish REE as Rosendal’s trusted horseback experience provider for visitors seeking memorable, structured countryside activities.

### Year-1 objectives
- Reach stable weekend booking cadence
- Achieve positive operational consistency
- Build repeat and referral demand from partner properties
- Maintain strict safety and welfare compliance

---

## 3) Market Context and Fit
Rosendal is a small-town destination; therefore REE is modeled around visitor flow rather than local resident volume.

### Demand sources
1. Weekend tourists
2. Guests already staying in Rosendal accommodations
3. Family day-trippers from nearby towns

### Why the model fits
- Activities complement accommodation stays
- Outdoor experiences align with Rosendal’s countryside identity
- The product can run at small scale with controlled costs

---

## 4) Product Portfolio
### Launch scope (first 6 months)
- Scenic Trail Ride (60 min)
- Sunset Signature Ride (75–90 min)
- Pony Discovery Ride (10–15 min)
- Private Couple Experience (pre-booked)

The initial portfolio is intentionally compact for quality and cost control.

---

## 5) Pricing Strategy (Reasonable and Recent)
### Recommended launch pricing
- Pony Discovery: **R120–R180**
- Scenic Trail (60 min): **R380–R520 per person**
- Sunset Signature: **R550–R750 per person**
- Private Couple Experience: **R1,300–R1,900 per booking**

### Pricing logic
- Maintain accessibility for startup adoption
- Protect contribution margins per product
- Use controlled adjustments based on occupancy and conversion data

---

## 6) Funding Ask and Allocation
## Total funding requested: **R280,000**

### Allocation framework
- 28% safety gear, tack, setup
- 20% horse care reserve
- 15% insurance and compliance
- 15% marketing + content + booking setup
- 12% working capital buffer
- 10% route/site readiness

This capital level is sufficient to launch professionally without overcapitalizing a small-market startup.

---

## 7) Operating Model
### Operating window
- Fridays to Sundays
- Public holidays
- Pre-booking priority to reduce no-show and idle costs

### Cost-control principles
- Keep fixed payroll lean
- Use demand-aligned scheduling
- Track per-product performance weekly

### Revenue model
Revenue = booked slots × product price + premium add-ons

---

## 8) Go-to-Market Plan
### Primary channel: Accommodation partners
- Formal referral relationships with local hosts
- Simple booking/referral workflow
- Reliable guest handling and communication

### Secondary channels
- WhatsApp direct booking
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram/Facebook content for discovery

### Conversion priorities
- Fast response time
- Booking clarity (time, what to bring, policy)
- Consistent guest experience and review capture

---

## 9) Safety, Compliance, Welfare
REE commits to non-negotiable operating standards:
- Indemnity and consent forms
- Rider suitability screening
- Mandatory safety briefings
- Incident response protocol
- Public liability insurance
- Horse care logs and workload controls
- Weather cancellation procedures

These controls are essential for investor confidence and sustainable operation.

---

## 10) Risk Management
### Key risks and mitigations
1. **Weather disruption**  
   Mitigation: reschedule policy and weather-buffered calendar.

2. **Demand variability**  
   Mitigation: partner-led funnel and flexible staffing.

3. **Safety incidents**  
   Mitigation: SOP adherence, training, documentation, insurance.

4. **Early cashflow pressure**  
   Mitigation: conservative cost structure and weekly cash tracking.

---

## 11) Milestone Plan (12 Months)
### Months 1–2
- Finalize legal/compliance pack
- Onboard launch partners
- Soft-launch with limited slot inventory

### Months 3–6
- Stabilize booking cadence
- Evaluate occupancy and product contribution margins
- Optimize pricing only where justified by data

### Months 7–12
- Improve repeat booking rates
- Launch one premium add-on if metrics support it
- Prepare a year-2 growth memo for investors

---

## 12) Investor Reporting Commitment
REE will provide:
- Monthly summary updates
- Quarterly performance dashboards
- Transparent use-of-funds tracking
- Risk and incident reporting protocol

---

## 13) Conclusion
REE is a premium-leaning but practical startup concept built for Rosendal’s market reality. The opportunity is not high-volume scale in year one; it is disciplined execution, strong service quality, controlled risk, and evidence-based growth.

For investors, this translates to a manageable capital entry point with a professional operating framework and a clear path to responsible expansion.
