# Startup Funding Proposal
## Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE)
### Rosendal, Free State, South Africa

**Date:** May 26, 2026  
**Purpose:** Secure startup funding for a small, practical tourism activity business.

---

## 1) Executive Summary
REE is a **small startup** focused on guided horse experiences for weekend visitors in Rosendal. The strategy is intentionally conservative: start small, control costs, prove demand, and grow only after stable monthly performance.

This proposal is designed for investors who want:
- Modest initial capital requirements
- Clear risk controls
- Realistic pricing aligned to South African activity market ranges
- Transparent milestones before scaling

---

## 2) Why This Can Work in Rosendal
Rosendal is a small town, so the business model is built around **visitor demand**, not local resident volume.

The model fits towns like Rosendal because:
- Visitors already come for a quiet countryside experience
- Accommodation operators need activity partners
- Horse experiences are memorable, visual, and suitable for families/couples

---

## 3) Startup Scope (Keep It Small)
## Phase 1 (first 6 months)
Launch with only 3 core products:
1. **Scenic Ride** (60 minutes)
2. **Sunset Ride** (75–90 minutes)
3. **Kids Pony Ride** (10–15 minutes)

**Operating days:** Friday to Sunday + public holidays  
**Approach:** Low fixed costs, pre-booking preferred, strict weather/safety controls

---

## 4) Pricing (Recent & Reasonable)
Pricing is benchmarked to recent publicly listed South African trail/pony offers (2025–2026), where 1-hour trail rides often fall around **R350–R750** and pony options are lower.

### Recommended launch pricing (Rosendal startup level)
- **Kids Pony Ride (10–15 min): R120–R180**
- **Scenic Ride (60 min): R380–R520 per person**
- **Sunset Ride (75–90 min): R550–R750 per person**
- **Private Couple Ride: R1,300–R1,900 per booking**

These are intentionally moderate to avoid overpricing for a small-town startup.

---

## 5) Lean Capital Requirement
## Funding ask (startup): **R280,000**

### Use of funds (practical split)
- 28% Safety gear, tack, and setup essentials
- 20% Horse care reserve (feed, vet, farrier buffer)
- 15% Insurance and compliance costs
- 15% Basic marketing + content + booking setup
- 12% Working capital buffer
- 10% Route/site preparation and signage

This budget is enough to start responsibly without creating scale pressure too early.

---

## 6) Monthly Cost Discipline (Target)
Key monthly cost buckets:
- Horse care
- Guide/support wages
- Fuel/transport
- Insurance
- Partner commissions (if referred bookings)
- Marketing and admin

**Control rule:** Keep fixed overhead low; use variable staffing around demand peaks.

---

## 7) Revenue Model (Simple)
Monthly revenue is based on booked slots only:
- Scenic bookings × scenic rate
- Sunset bookings × sunset rate
- Pony rides × pony rate
- Private rides × package rate

### Unit economics rule
Do not expand products that consistently produce weak contribution margins.

---

## 8) Partnership-Led Sales Plan
Primary channel: Rosendal accommodation partners.

- Give each partner a clear booking/referral process
- Offer fair referral commissions (e.g., 10–20% depending on volume)
- Focus on same-day response and reliable guest handling

Secondary channels:
- WhatsApp bookings
- Google Business Profile
- Instagram/Facebook content

---

## 9) Safety, Legal, and Welfare (Non-Negotiable)
Before commercial launch:
- Signed indemnity forms
- Rider screening (age/size/confidence)
- Pre-ride safety briefing
- Incident procedure
- Public liability cover
- Vet/farrier/welfare logs
- Weather cancellation policy

Small business does not mean small safety standards.

---

## 10) Risk & Mitigation (Realistic)
1. **Low-demand weekends**  
   Mitigation: Flexible staffing, partner bundles, seasonal offers.

2. **Bad weather disruptions**  
   Mitigation: Clear reschedule terms and weather buffers.

3. **Safety incidents**  
   Mitigation: SOPs, training, conservative rider matching, insurance.

4. **Cashflow pressure early months**  
   Mitigation: Lean cost base, strict cash tracking, phased spending.

---

## 11) Milestones (First 12 Months)
### Months 1–2
- Finalize compliance, forms, and SOPs
- Confirm first accommodation partners
- Soft launch limited slots

### Months 3–6
- Stabilize weekend operations
- Track demand by product and partner
- Adjust pricing only after data

### Months 7–12
- Improve occupancy and repeat bookings
- Add one premium add-on only if margins support it
- Prepare small expansion case for year 2

---

## 12) Investor Offer
REE is seeking startup funding of **R280,000** to launch responsibly and prove the model in Rosendal.

Investors receive:
- Monthly operational updates
- Quarterly performance summary
- Milestone-based spending discipline
- A realistic growth path without aggressive assumptions

---

## Closing Note
This is a **practical startup**, not a large-scale tourism rollout. The plan is intentionally right-sized for Rosendal: begin small, run professionally, protect downside, and scale only when the numbers justify it.
