# Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE)
## Premium Startup Concept Note

**Document version:** 2.0 (Professional Edition)  
**Date:** May 26, 2026  
**Location:** Rosendal, Free State, South Africa

---

## 1) Executive Concept
Rosendal Equestrian Experiences (REE) is a professionally positioned, small-scale tourism startup that delivers curated horseback experiences for weekend visitors, couples, and families in Rosendal.

The concept is intentionally right-sized for a small town: lean launch costs, premium service standards, strict safety governance, and phased growth only after performance proof.

**Positioning statement:**  
*“A boutique countryside riding experience in Rosendal: safe, memorable, and professionally run.”*

---

## 2) The Market Opportunity (Small Town, Smart Model)
Rosendal’s scale requires a visitor-led model rather than local-volume dependence.

REE is built around:
- **Weekend tourism demand** (Fri–Sun concentration)
- **Accommodation-linked referrals** (guesthouses/lodges/retreats)
- **Experience-first spend behavior** (tourists seeking memorable activities)

This creates a realistic opportunity for a niche operator that focuses on quality over volume.

---

## 3) Core Offering Portfolio
### Launch products (Phase 1)
1. **Scenic Trail Ride** (60 minutes)  
   Guided route designed for relaxed leisure riders.
2. **Sunset Signature Ride** (75–90 minutes)  
   Premium golden-hour experience with curated stop.
3. **Pony Discovery Ride** (10–15 minutes)  
   Family-focused product for younger children.
4. **Private Couple Experience** (by booking)  
   Premium upsell for anniversaries/special occasions.

---

## 4) Premium Service Standards
REE will differentiate through disciplined execution:
- Structured guest communication from booking to post-ride
- Clean rider onboarding and pre-ride briefings
- Professional guide conduct and experience narration
- Route consistency and punctual start times
- Post-ride feedback capture for quality assurance

---

## 5) Pricing Architecture (Professional, Reasonable)
Recommended launch pricing (conservative but premium-leaning):

- **Pony Discovery Ride:** R120–R180
- **Scenic Trail Ride (60m):** R380–R520 per person
- **Sunset Signature Ride:** R550–R750 per person
- **Private Couple Experience:** R1,300–R1,900 per booking

Pricing is structured to remain accessible while supporting sustainable margins.

---

## 6) Startup Funding Requirement
## Total startup ask: **R280,000**

### Use of funds (disciplined split)
- 28% Safety gear, tack, and launch setup
- 20% Horse care reserve (feed, vet, farrier)
- 15% Insurance and compliance
- 15% Marketing, content, and booking setup
- 12% Working capital protection
- 10% Route readiness and signage

The funding structure is intentionally modest to reduce investor risk while enabling professional launch quality.

---

## 7) Go-to-Market Model
### Primary channel: Partner properties
- Guesthouses and retreats become referral partners.
- Clear booking workflow and service reliability build partner confidence.

### Secondary channels:
- Google Business Profile
- WhatsApp bookings
- Instagram/Facebook visual storytelling

This blended channel model supports consistent discovery and conversion.

---

## 8) Safety, Legal, and Welfare Governance
REE will operate with non-negotiable controls:
- Signed indemnity documentation
- Rider suitability screening
- Mandatory pre-ride safety briefing
- Incident response process
- Public liability cover
- Horse welfare logs and rest-cycle controls
- Weather cancellation/reschedule policy

---

## 9) Execution Roadmap (First 12 Months)
### Months 1–2: Foundation
- Finalize SOPs, forms, and insurance
- Onboard launch accommodation partners
- Soft-launch limited slots

### Months 3–6: Stabilization
- Improve occupancy on weekend slots
- Track conversion by partner and product
- Adjust pricing only from real booking data

### Months 7–12: Optimization
- Improve repeat bookings and referrals
- Add one premium add-on only if contribution margins remain healthy
- Prepare year-2 growth plan from evidence

---

## 10) Investor Value Proposition
REE offers a fundable profile for small-capital investors:
- Modest startup ask
- Clear cost controls
- Defined risk management
- Professional service differentiation
- Phased expansion discipline

**Bottom line:** REE is a premium-quality, small-town startup concept designed for practical execution and responsible growth.
